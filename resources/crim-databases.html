💡 Tips for CRIM107 Victimology:
  • Start with ProQuest Criminal Justice for victimology-specific articles
  • SAGE Journals has strong criminology coverage (many victimology journals)
  • ANZ Journal of Criminology for Aus-specific victimology research
  • Trends and Issues (AIC) for Australian policy context
  • PsycINFO for psychological dimensions of victimisation
  • Cross-reference with Scopus to find most-cited papers in your area
