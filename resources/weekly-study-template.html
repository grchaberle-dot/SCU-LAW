📋 Weekly Unit Study Session

Eddie's System (Coaching Session 2 of 6)

← Back to Hub
🎯 Duplicate this process for each week of each unit. The goal: never go into a lecture cold.

1 Pre-Lecture Preparation

  • Copy LMS weekly goals/achievables (this is your compass for the week)
  • Create headings from lecture slides contents page or themes you identify
  • Read the abridged text first (student companion/pocket guide, NOT the full textbook)
  • Identify what to listen for: Tests to apply, Scope (subjective vs objective), Steps/elements, Available defences
💡 You're creating a skeleton of notes BEFORE the lecture. During the lecture you just slot info under existing headings.

2 During Lecture: Structured Notes

  • Don't write everything. Slot information under your pre-made headings.
  • You're HUNTING for content, not transcribing.
  • Note any assessment hints/tips the lecturer drops.
  • Flag anything that doesn't fit your headings (add a new heading if needed).

3 Post-Lecture: AI Processing

  • Use notetaker AI to process the recording
  • Generate: lecture summary, key cases & legislation table, flashcards, quiz, diagrams
  • Use the Case Law Summarisation Prompt for each case mentioned
  • Verify AI outputs against primary sources (AI hallucinates)

4 Tutorial Notes

  • Note tutor name and whether they mark your assessment
  • Prepare 3 questions to ask in tute
  • Ask: "What does the marker want to see in the assessment?"
  • Record activity/case study notes from tute

5 Weekly Readings: Hunt Mode

  • You're not "reading." You're hunting for content that fits under your note headings.
  • AI summarise cases first, then verify against primary source
  • Extract rules/principles and slot into Section 2 headings
🎯 Prescribed readings are there to support your headings. Hunt the content, don't passively read cover to cover.

6 Actionable Items & Follow-ups

  • What needs doing before next week?
  • Cases to brief?
  • Assessment pieces to work on?
  • Questions to clarify?
