📚 Legal Research Databases

UNE Access - Your gateway to case law, legislation & journals

🏛️ Primary Legal Databases

Westlaw AU (Thomson Reuters)

Cases, legislation, journals, commentary. The big one for Australian law.

westlaw.com.au

UNE Library login required

LexisNexis AU

Cases, legislation, encyclopedias (Halsbury's), CaseBase citator, journals.

lexisnexis.com.au

UNE Library login required

AustLII (Free)

Australasian Legal Information Institute. Free access to cases, legislation, treaties from all Australian jurisdictions.

austlii.edu.au

Free - no login needed

Jade (Free)

AI-enhanced legal research. Cases with paragraph-level citations, visual case maps.

jade.io

Free account - enhanced features with UNE email

Federal Register of Legislation

Official Commonwealth legislation (Acts, regulations, compilations). Authoritative versions.

legislation.gov.au

Free - no login needed

NSW Legislation

Official NSW Acts, regulations, EPIs. Includes historical versions and point-in-time access.

legislation.nsw.gov.au

Free - no login needed

📖 Encyclopedias & Commentary

Halsbury's Laws of Australia

Comprehensive legal encyclopedia covering all areas of Australian law. Great starting point for research.

Access via LexisNexis AU

UNE Library login required

Laws of Australia (Thomson Reuters)

Encyclopedic commentary by topic. Alternative to Halsbury's.

Access via Westlaw AU

UNE Library login required

Encyclopaedia Britannica

General encyclopedia useful for background context on legal concepts, historical events, and key figures.

britannica.com

Free access + premium via UNE Library

📝 Journals & Articles

HeinOnline

Massive law journal archive. Image-based PDFs of journals going back decades. Essential for legal history research.

heinonline.org

UNE Library login required

Informit (RMIT)

Australian journal articles, including legal journals. AGIS Plus Text database.

informit.org

UNE Library login required
