🏛️ Primary Legal Databases

Westlaw AU (Thomson Reuters)

Cases, legislation, journals, commentary. The big one for Australian law. Structured case presentation with notes/explanations.

UNE access includes: FirstPoint (case citator). Does NOT include: case summaries or AI-assisted search.

westlaw.com.au

UNE Library login required

LexisNexis AU

Cases, legislation, encyclopedias (Halsbury's), CaseBase citator, journals.

lexisnexis.com.au

UNE Library login required

AustLII (Free)

Australasian Legal Information Institute. Free access to cases, legislation, treaties from all Australian jurisdictions. Great click-through to sections.

austlii.edu.au

Free - no login needed⚠️ May lag behind updates

Jade (Free)

AI-enhanced legal research. Cases with paragraph-level citations, visual case maps. Clean layout for reading full judgments.

jade.io

Free account - enhanced features with UNE email⚠️ Verify currency against official sites

📜 Official Legislation Sources

NSW Legislation (Primary Source for NSW)

Official NSW Acts, regulations, EPIs. Includes historical versions via the version date selector (timeline bar on any Act page).

Key features: Browse tabs (In Force, Repealed, As Made, Bills), alphabet browse, version history.

Bills tab: Find Explanatory Notes explaining WHY provisions changed. Essential for statutory interpretation.

legislation.nsw.gov.au | /browse

Free - no login needed

Federal Register of Legislation

Official Commonwealth legislation (Acts, regulations, compilations). Authoritative current versions.

legislation.gov.au

Free - no login needed

Other State Legislation Sites

Same structure as NSW. Always go to the official site for the relevant jurisdiction.

VIC: legislation.vic.gov.au | QLD: legislation.qld.gov.au

Free - no login needed

NSW Hansard (Second Reading Speeches)

Parliamentary debates including Second Reading Speeches. Use for statutory interpretation when you need to understand Parliament's intent behind a provision.

parliament.nsw.gov.au/hansard

Free - no login needed

🛠️ Research Workflows

🔍 Finding Legislation

  1. Identify jurisdiction (NSW, Cth, other state)
  2. Go to official legislation site for that jurisdiction
  3. Browse alphabetically or search by name/topic
  4. Check you have the correct VERSION (use date selector if historical)
  5. Read: Preliminaries → Definitions → Relevant Part/Division → Schedules
  6. Check for associated Regulations (99% of Acts have them)
  7. If interpreting: check Bills tab for Explanatory Notes + Hansard for Second Reading Speeches

📚 Reading Case Law (When No Summary Available)

  1. Know what you're looking for BEFORE opening the case
  2. Find the case on Jade or AustLII (free) or Westlaw (uni access)
  3. Copy full text into AI with case summary prompt (extract: admin history, parties, facts, issues, arguments, decision)
  4. Read AI summary first, THEN skim actual judgment (you'll get through it much faster)
  5. Identify: ratio decidendi (binding principle) + obiter dicta (persuasive commentary)
  6. Note prior authorities cited (useful for building your own arguments)
  7. Always distinguish the court's DECISION from its ORDERS (they can differ!)
💡 Pro tips from Tutor Session 3:
• Always verify AustLII/Jade against official legislation sites for currency
• Use the version of the Act in force at the TIME OF CONDUCT, not today's version
• Definitions in an Act may only apply to the Part they appear in
• Watch for determinative words: AND, OR, MUST, MAY, UNLESS
• Don't stop reading after finding elements: defences/exceptions often follow below
• Stick to lecturer-provided cases only: don't expand the case list yourself
