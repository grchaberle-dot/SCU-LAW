Courts/precedent
The Australian Constitution (1901) is the supreme law. It creates the federal structure, distributes power, and cannot be changed without a s128 referendum (majority of voters + majority of states).
Legislation/statutes made by Parliament (Cth or State). Passed through both Houses + Royal Assent. Delegated legislation made under authority of an Act. Prevails over common law (parliamentary sovereignty).
Judge-made law through decisions (precedent). Doctrine of stare decisis: lower courts bound by higher courts in same hierarchy. Ratio decidendi = binding principle. Obiter dicta = persuasive only.

Doctrine of Precedent (Stare Decisis)

Ratio Decidendi

  • The legal REASON for the decision
  • Binding on lower courts in same hierarchy
  • The principle that must be followed
  • Found by identifying material facts + legal reasoning

Obiter Dicta

  • 'Said in passing'
  • NOT binding, only persuasive
  • May be followed if from a respected court/judge
  • Hypothetical scenarios discussed by judge

Statutory Interpretation: Approaches

Literal Rule

Words given ordinary, plain meaning. Even if result seems unjust. Most formalist approach.

Purposive Approach

Interpret to give effect to Parliament's PURPOSE. s15AA Acts Interpretation Act 1901 (Cth). Now dominant in Australia.

Golden Rule: Use literal meaning UNLESS it produces absurdity, then adopt alternative meaning.
Mischief Rule: What 'mischief' (problem) was the Act designed to remedy? Interpret to suppress the mischief.

Only Commonwealth can legislate: Customs/excise (s90), currency, defence forces raised by Cth, territories (s122), seat of government.
Both can legislate, but s109: If inconsistency, Cth prevails. Includes: trade/commerce, taxation, banking, marriage, immigration, corporations, external affairs.
