🏗️ 5. Deprivation vs Importation Models

🔒 Deprivation Model (Sykes 1958)

  • Prison culture develops IN RESPONSE to deprivation
  • "Pains of imprisonment" drive behaviour
  • Loss of: liberty, goods, relationships, autonomy, security
  • Argot roles emerge as coping
  • Violence = adaptation to environment

📥 Importation Model

  • Pre-prison lives IMPORTED in
  • Criminal history matters most
  • Gang membership carries over
  • Substance abuse patterns continue
  • Violence = continuation of outside behaviour
DiIulio (1987): Both models miss the point. Prison MANAGEMENT is the key variable.
